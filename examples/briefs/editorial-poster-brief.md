# Sample Creative Brief: Architectural Monograph Poster

## Project Overview

Design an exhibition poster announcing an autumn monograph lecture series on tactile timber and concrete architecture.

## Audience & Medium

- **Medium**: Portrait print poster (`A2` / `3:4` aspect ratio) and social announcement crop.
- **Audience**: Architects, industrial designers, editorial print collectors.
- **Tone**: Quiet authority, material warmth, disciplined geometry.

## Constraints

- Maximum three colors (one dark neutral ground, one warm paper tone, one subtle accent).
- Must keep at least 45% open breathing room so venue information remains effortlessly scannable from a distance.
- Avoid glossy gradients, decorative badges, or multi-font novelty.
