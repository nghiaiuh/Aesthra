# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Aesthra a respectful, constructive, and welcoming experience for everyone regardless of background, discipline, or level of experience.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate clearly, respectfully, and in good faith during reviews and discussions.
- Focus critiques on ideas, creative principles, and engineering quality rather than individuals.
- Respect licensing boundaries, attribution, and creative rights.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, exclusionary remarks, publishing private information, or knowingly submitting misattributed or infringing third-party media assets is not acceptable.

## Reporting

Project maintainers may be contacted via the repository's public GitHub issue tracker or repository moderation mechanisms to address violations fairly and proportionally.
